## How I think about product in fintech

1. **Compliance is a design constraint, not a legal department problem** — the best fintech features are built around regulation, not retrofitted for it
2. **Approval rate is a product metric** — payment failure is a UX failure. I've debugged Apple Pay, 3DS, cross-border SEPA issues at the product level
3. **Distribution beats features** — OFP reached 180 countries without a single sales hire. Affiliate systems, community, and word of mouth are product decisions
